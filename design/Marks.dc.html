Six marks
All 8×8, full bleed, no gaps. Each is a fixed pattern, not random at runtime, so whichever you pick stays that pattern forever.
A. Balanced static
Even 32 of 64. The default — reads as noise at every size.
B. Sparse
Lighter, calmer. Holds up small but reads less like static.
C. Dense
Heavier, more aggressive. Can clog below about 20px.
D. Four-tone
Closer to real CRT static. Richer large, muddier small.
E. Resolving
Noisy at the top, quiet at the bottom — what the panel actually does.
F. One critical
Balanced static with a single red cell. The one problem.
