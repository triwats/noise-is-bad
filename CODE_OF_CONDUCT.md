# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Say what you mean without
being unpleasant about it.

## What that means here

Disagreement is fine and useful; this project has strong opinions and they
should be argued with. Rudeness, personal remarks, harassment and sustained
disruption are not fine, whatever the technical point being made.

Nobody here owes you their time. Maintainers may not answer quickly, and a
change may be declined because it pulls against what the project is for rather
than because it is bad.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer, privately: <https://github.com/triwats>.

Reports are handled quietly. Depending on what happened, the outcome may be a
word in private, an edited or removed comment, or being blocked from the
repository. Whoever is handling a report stays out of it if they are involved.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else this project is the
subject and someone is representing it.
